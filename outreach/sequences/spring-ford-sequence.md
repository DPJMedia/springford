# Spring-Ford Press — advertiser outreach sequence

**Deliverability first — read before sending:**
- Do **NOT** send from `admin@dpjmedia.com` (your newsletter + Stripe-receipt sender). Cold
  outreach complaints there will hurt the inbox placement of your real transactional mail.
- Use a **separate sending domain** (e.g. `dpjmedia-media.com`) + a dedicated inbox. Warm it
  up before volume. Start at ~20–30/day sent by hand; scale later with a tool (Instantly/Smartlead).
- Every email must include a real mailing address and a one-line opt-out (CAN-SPAM).

**Merge fields** (columns from the lead CSV): `{{business_name}}`, `{{town}}`, `{{category}}`,
`{{contact_name}}` (blank → use "there"), plus `{{custom_line}}` (one sentence written from
their website — this is what lifts reply rates; can be AI-generated later).

**The pitch, in one line:** *the paper ~33,000 local people read every month, advertising your
business to the exact neighbors who walk past your door.*

---

## For the two new sites (Pottstown / Phoenixville)

These have almost no traffic yet, so **don't quote reach numbers** — sell the ground floor:

> "We just launched Pottstown Press. I'm signing a handful of **founding local sponsors** at a
> locked-in flat rate before we grow — you'd be one of the first names in front of readers as we
> ramp up coverage in town."

Bundle option worth testing: *"advertise across all three of our Schuylkill-Valley papers —
Spring-Ford, Pottstown, and Phoenixville — for one combined rate."*
